# Speaking at Cloud Native Karachi

A talk is worth 2 [contribution units](../governance/membership.md#contribution-units), most of the way to Membership. We are shorter of speakers than of slots.

Open a [talk proposal](https://github.com/cloud-native-karachi/community/issues/new?template=talk-proposal.yml). The relevant [SIG Lead](../sigs/README.md) and one organizer review it, usually within 14 days.

## Vendor neutrality

A CNCF condition of being a community group, not a preference of ours.

**Not acceptable:** a walkthrough of your company's product or console; a talk whose conclusion is to buy or evaluate a specific commercial thing; a sales pitch in any form, including a technical deep dive that becomes one at slide 20; speaking because your employer sponsored the event.

**Acceptable and wanted:** a talk about an open source CNCF project, including one your employer maintains; a neutral comparison of competing tools, commercial ones included, that reaches an honest conclusion; a production war story where the lesson transfers; how you solved a real problem, naming whatever tools you used.

The test: **would this talk still be useful to someone who will never buy what you sell?** If the value evaporates without the product, it is a pitch. Reviewers will tell you which side you landed on and what would move you across.

Nobody is barred for who employs them. Organizers here work for a consultancy that sells cloud services, so a rule excluding vendor employees would exclude us. The rule is about the talk.

## Formats

| Format | Length | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lightning talk | 10 min, no questions | First-time speakers, one idea, a tool you like |
| Talk | 25 to 30 min plus 5 for questions | The standard slot |
| Workshop or lab | 60 to 90 min | Hands-on, needs room and wifi checked in advance |
| Panel | 40 min | Only with a prepared moderator |

Unsure? Propose a lightning talk. Ten minutes on one idea beats thirty stretched over it.

## First-time speakers

Say so in your proposal. It helps rather than hurts, and it gets you a dry run with an organizer a week before, help cutting the abstract's scope, a lightning slot if you would rather start small, and someone in the front row keeping time.

Take the dry run. It is where you find out the talk is 40 minutes, not 25.

We would rather run a slightly rough talk from someone new than a polished one from the same three people every quarter.

## What we ask

- **Confirm, then turn up.** A cancellation inside 48 hours is the worst thing that can happen to an event. Tell the owner the moment you know; life happens, silence is the problem.
- **25 minutes means 25 minutes.** Overrunning eats the next speaker's slot, not the break.
- **Send slides the day before** as a backup on the event laptop, so a dead laptop is an inconvenience rather than a cancelled talk.
- **Share slides afterwards** for the retro. A public link is preferred, a PDF is fine.
- **No live credentials or client data.** Demos run against a throwaway environment. Anonymise anything from work and check you are allowed to give it. [sig-security](../sigs/sig-security/charter.md) has a standing rule that applies everywhere.
- **Bring your own adapter.** The venue will not have the one you need.

## What you get

An audience that works with this stuff, the talk recorded and published if you consent, 2 contribution units, and a step toward Membership and leading a SIG.

Also: the fastest way to be taken seriously in this field locally is to have given a good talk about something you understand.
