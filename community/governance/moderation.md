# Moderation

For the organizer handling a Code of Conduct report. Reporting something? See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

The governing document is the [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

## Scope and limits

We can act on our own spaces: events and venues, `#cloud-native-karachi`, both WhatsApp groups, our repositories, our social accounts.

We cannot ban anyone from CNCF Slack as a workspace, from other community groups, from KubeCon, or from upstream projects. Anything that should follow a person across the wider community goes to <conduct@cncf.io>. Do not tell a reporter we handled something when what we did was remove one person from one WhatsApp group.

## Handling a report

1. **Acknowledge within 3 days** and say who is handling it.
2. **Determine recusal first.** Any organizer named in the report, close to anyone named, or with a commercial interest in the outcome, recuses, and it is recorded. If that leaves fewer than 2 organizers, the report goes to the CNCF. One person does not decide alone.
3. **Gather facts** from the reporter, the person reported, and witnesses. The person reported is told what is alleged and responds before any decision beyond a protective measure. Notes go in the private record.
4. **Decide.** Majority of non-recused organizers.
5. **Tell the reporter the outcome**, never details identifying another reporter.
6. **Record** date, allegation, who decided, who recused, the remedy, and any expiry date.

Target 14 days. If it will take longer, say so and why.

## Remedies

Skip up the list where the conduct warrants it.

| Remedy | Decided by | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Informal word | 1 organizer | Misunderstandings. Not a Code of Conduct action. |
| Written warning | Majority of non-recused | Recorded, with what happens on a repeat. |
| Content removal | 1 organizer, immediately | Slurs, harassment, doxxing, spam. Report to the others after. |
| Removal from a channel, 30 to 90 days | Majority | Has an end date. Someone has to re-add them. |
| Removal from the register | Majority | Emeritus with a private note. Register shows no reason. |
| Removal as SIG Lead or Organizer | [organizers.md](organizers.md#stepping-down-and-offboarding) | Triggers credential rotation. |
| Barred from events, indefinite | Unanimous among non-recused | Also referred to the CNCF. |
| Referral to the CNCF | 1 organizer, any time | Never blocked locally, never a substitute for acting locally. |

**Protective measures** may be taken by one organizer without a vote where safety is at stake: remove from a channel, ask someone to leave, delete content. Report within 24 hours, confirmed or reversed within 7 days. It is not a finding.

## Confidentiality

Reports stay with the non-recused organizers handling them. We do not share a reporter's identity with the person reported without asking first, and we do not discuss live reports in the channels or with anyone's employer.

Mail to <cloudnative.karachi@outlook.com> is part of that record. It is a group account in the shared credential store rather than an organizer's own inbox, at least 2 organizers can read it, and access moves at [offboarding](organizers.md#credential-rotation-within-7-days-of-the-pull-request-merging).

The private record lives in the shared credential store, reachable by at least 2 organizers, so a pattern across reports is visible to whoever handles the next one. Confirm it is not held only by a departing organizer, per [offboarding step 7](organizers.md#credential-rotation-within-7-days-of-the-pull-request-merging).

Register entries never carry a reason. Emeritus after a decision looks identical to emeritus for inactivity, because the alternative is a permanent public punishment we did not decide to impose.

## Appeals

Within 30 days, to the organizers, or to <conduct@cncf.io> if the appeal concerns organizers.

Heard by non-recused organizers who did not decide the original matter. Where none exists, which a roster this small makes likely, it goes to the CNCF. Do not have the same two people hear the appeal against their own decision.

An appeal can present new information or argue the process was not followed. It does not pause the remedy unless organizers agree.

## Conflicts we should expect

Organizers share an employer, and a group this size has overlapping relationships throughout.

Recusal will sometimes leave us without a quorum. Refer to the CNCF rather than proceeding, and write that as the reason.

An organizer with a commercial relationship to someone in a report, as client, employer or prospect, recuses on that basis alone. The test is what the record looks like to the reporter, not how impartial the organizer feels.
