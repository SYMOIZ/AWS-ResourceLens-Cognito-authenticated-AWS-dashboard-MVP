# Code of Conduct

Cloud Native Karachi follows the [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md), which is the governing document. This page is only who to contact and what happens next.

It applies to meetups and workshops, `#cloud-native-karachi` on CNCF Slack, both WhatsApp groups, all Cloud Native Karachi repositories, our social accounts, and any venue or side conversation attached to an event.

## Reporting

Report to the organizers, to the CNCF, or both. You are never required to come to us first.

| Route | Where | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Local** | <cloudnative.karachi@outlook.com>, or DM any **two** organizers from [organizers.yaml](members/organizers.yaml) on CNCF Slack | Anything in our own spaces. Two organizers, so no single one is a bottleneck and you can route around one who is involved |
| **CNCF** | <conduct@cncf.io>, or <eventconduct@cncf.io> for a CNCF-produced event | Anything involving an organizer, anything you would rather we did not handle, anything serious. They respond within three business days and support anonymous reporting |

That mailbox is a group account, not one person's inbox. It is held in the group credential store and read by at least two organizers, so a report does not sit unread while somebody is on leave or is the person it concerns. If your report is about an organizer and you would rather it did not reach them at all, use the CNCF route.

Whatever you have is enough to start: what happened, when, where, who was involved, whether anyone else saw it, any screenshots. If you do not have all of that, report anyway.

## What happens next

We acknowledge within 3 days and say who is handling it. Any organizer named in a report, or close to someone named, recuses from handling it and from the decision.

Reports stay confidential to the organizers handling them. We will not share your identity with the person you reported without asking you first. We will tell you what action we took, though not details identifying another reporter.

Remedies run from a warning to removal from a channel or the register to being barred from events; the full list and the escalation path are in [governance/moderation.md](governance/moderation.md). We cannot act outside our own group, so anything that should follow a person across the wider cloud native community goes to the CNCF.

Retaliation for a good-faith report is itself a violation, and a serious one.
